Where each dock icon comes from (2026-10-27):

netflix.svg, appletv.svg, spotify.svg, youtube.svg, ps5.svg (PlayStation logo on PlayStation blue)
  Logo shapes from Simple Icons 16.36.0 (CC0), https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.36.0/icons/
  placed on the brand's app-icon background (Netflix N in its two official reds on black, Apple logo
  white on black, Spotify green on black, YouTube red on white).
prime.svg
  "Amazon Prime Video logo (2024).svg" from Wikimedia Commons (the 2024 app icon), unchanged.
disney.png, partner.png, stremio.png
  The apps' own icons, copied from the TV (/opt/usr/apps/<package>/shared/res/).

The logos are the brands' trademarks; used here only in Noam and Amir’s own TV remote.
